The Story

From the sales floor
to the screen.

I didn't start with a camera or a content calendar. I started with a sales target — and a feeling that I was meant to build something more creative.

Prologue11 Months

The Sales Floor

Junior Sales Consultant

Right after my studies, I spent 11 months in sales. Every day I spoke to real customers, heard what they wanted, and learned why people say yes. It taught me the one thing every marketer needs: a brand is really a conversation with people. But I didn't want to only deliver the message. I wanted to create it.

The TurnTraining

Starting Over

Digital Marketing · Monz Creative School (PUMO Technovation), Thudiyalur

So I made a bold choice and started over. I joined the Digital Marketing course at Monz Creative School (PUMO Technovation) in Thudiyalur, Coimbatore — SEO, social media, content and ads. And I didn't wait for the course to end before I got to work.

Act IFreelance

Outing Constructions

Social Media Content Strategist

While still in class, I landed my first brand. I learned by doing — studying competitors, planning content and building a 90-day structure the team could follow every day. Theory in the morning, real work at night.

Act IIFreelance

DAHLYA

Digital Marketing Executive — Full Scope

Next, a boutique brand trusted me with its entire digital presence — the content, the social pages and the way the brand looked and spoke online. My first time owning the whole picture.

Act IIINow Showing

YouGoTrip Current

Senior Digital Marketing Executive

Today I lead digital marketing at YouGoTrip — planning campaigns, designing creatives and editing films that make people want to pack their bags. From sales trainee to senior executive, one brand at a time.
